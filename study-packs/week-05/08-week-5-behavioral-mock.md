# Week 5 Behavioral Mock

**Format:** 45 minutes, 6 questions, partner preferred. Score with `00-project/learning-roadmap.md` §8.4 (Behavioral Communication rubric).

## Part A — Candidate script

Deliver six stories, each in ≤ 2 minutes, drawn from your bank of 8 (Weeks 1, 2, 4) — at least two must be delivered using their scope-reframed version from `07-story-scope-reframing.md`, not the original Senior-scope telling.

1. An architecture decision (Story 1)
2. A technical disagreement (Story 2)
3. A production incident (Story 3)
4. Technical debt you argued for (Story 4)
5. Cross-team influence (Story 7) — **deliver the scope-reframed version**
6. A migration you led (Story 8) — **deliver the scope-reframed version**

## Part B — Interviewer script

For each story, ask exactly one follow-up before moving on:

1. "What would you have done if the alternative had won instead?"
2. "What was the other person's strongest argument, in their own words?"
3. "What did you personally do in the first hour?"
4. "What was the business case, in terms a non-engineer would accept?"
5. "Who did you have to convince, and what was their objection?" *(if this isn't already answered by the reframed telling, that's the specific gap to flag)*
6. "What would you do differently if you led this migration again today?"

**Scoring note:** stories 5 and 6 specifically should be evaluated against whether the scope-reframing actually surfaced organizational impact and a real represented objection — a reframe that still ends at "and my team benefited" without naming who else was affected hasn't actually completed the T-1503 exercise, regardless of how polished the delivery is.
