# Week 9 — How the Work Itself Gets Organized

## Weekly Outcome

By the end of this week you can correctly use the vocabulary every team actually works in day to day — sprint, standup, backlog, story point, Scrum vs. Kanban vs. Waterfall — and explain why most real teams use a blend rather than a textbook-pure version of any one methodology.

## Why This Week Matters

Every prior week taught you to build and ship (Week 8's Spring/REST/Docker capstone). This short closing week teaches the process vocabulary that organizes *how* that work actually gets planned and tracked on a real team — knowledge assumed by every day-to-day conversation a new engineer has in their first week on the job, but never explicitly taught anywhere else in this path.

## Prerequisites

None beyond Week 8 — this topic is independent of the technical material and was placed last deliberately, once there's a real shipped project to reference when discussing how its own work would have been planned.

## Behavioral Exercise

Describe, in STAR form, how the Week 8 capstone project (the Spring Boot REST API you built) would have been broken into sprints/stories if built on a real team — this connects the process vocabulary directly to work you actually did, not an abstract scenario.

## Mock Interview
