# Week 8 — Advocacy and the Review Process Itself

## Weekly Outcome

By the end of this week you can build and defend a prioritization case for paying down technical debt against competing feature work, and you can describe how you'd shape a technical decision through the design-review or RFC process itself rather than only through your own individual contribution.

## Why This Week Matters

This pack's final week closes two loops opened earlier. The learning path calls [Technical Debt: Prioritization and Advocacy](../../../syllabus/19-leadership-staff/technical-debt-prioritization-and-advocacy.md) "the organizational-advocacy half of Topic 5's technical framing" — Week 3's economic framing of debt, now argued to people who control the roadmap. It calls [Design Reviews and RFCs as an Organizational Practice](../../../syllabus/19-leadership-staff/design-reviews-and-rfcs-as-organizational-practice.md) "shaping decisions through the review process itself, at scale" — the organizational mechanism behind Week 3's Architecture Decision Records.

Weeks 1–7 of this pack, in particular Week 3 (Technical Debt and Evolutionary Architecture; Architecture Decision Records).
