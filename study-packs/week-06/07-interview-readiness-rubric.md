## Targets

| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| Technical depth | 4+ follow-ups sustained on 3 of 4 sampled topics |
| Coding | Medium in ≤ 25 min, narrated, correct first submission ≥ 60% |
| System design | Six phases unprompted; ≥ 3 failure modes; ≥ 2 explicit trade-offs |
| Behavioral | 8 stories at 2 min; every one quantified; every one first-person |
| Java fluency | No syntax hesitation; correct collection choice justified aloud |
| Production judgment | Every design component paired with a real failure mode |
| **Delta vs Day 0** | **Measurable improvement on all six D1–D4 artifacts** |

## Scoring against Day 0

Use `05-diagnostic-rerun.md`'s comparison table directly — the Delta-vs-Day-0 row above is not a separate measurement, it *is* that table, restated as a pass/fail check. "Measurable" means a specific, describable difference in structure or content (as in `06-week-6-assessment-deliverable.md`'s worked example), not a felt sense of "I think I did better."

## What to do if the bar isn't met

Unlike Week 3, there is no scheduled Week 7 to fall back into within Plan A — this assessment lands at the edge of the 6-week sprint. If the bar isn't met:

- **If interviews are already scheduled:** proceed anyway — interviews themselves are practice, and the honest self-assessment from this week tells you which 2–3 upcoming interviews to treat as calibration rather than as the ones you most need to land, prioritizing higher-preference companies later in your actual interview sequence.
- **If interviews are not yet scheduled:** this is the natural rollover point into Plan B (`00-project/learning-roadmap.md` §4) — Weeks 1–6 of Plan B are identical to Plan A, so nothing here is wasted; Week 7 onward broadens into Spring depth/security, Kafka, concurrency/JVM, and the topics this sprint deliberately deferred.
