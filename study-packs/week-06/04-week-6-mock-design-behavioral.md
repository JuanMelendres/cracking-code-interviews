# Week 6 Mock — Design + Behavioral

**Format:** 60-min design on a genuinely unseen problem, 45-min behavioral. Partner strongly preferred — an unseen design problem specifically requires a partner to supply it.

## Table of Contents

1. [Part A — Candidate script](#part-a--candidate-script)
2. [Part B — Interviewer script](#part-b--interviewer-script)

## Part A — Candidate script

**Design (60 min):** run the full six-phase method (`study-packs/week-03/03-system-design-method.md`) on a problem your partner supplies that is **not** ride-hailing, news feed, or payment processing (this program's three practiced designs). Suggested unseen problems: a URL shortener with analytics, a collaborative whiteboard, a hotel-booking system, a distributed rate limiter. Draw on any relevant chapter across Weeks 1–5 as needed — caching, failure modes, decomposition, CAP, idempotency are all fair game depending on what the problem calls for.

**Behavioral (45 min):** deliver all 8 stories, each in ≤ 2 minutes, cold — no re-reading beforehand. At least Stories 1, 4, 7, 8 should come out in their scope-reframed form from `study-packs/week-05/07-story-scope-reframing.md` without needing to consciously recall "use the reframed version."

## Part B — Interviewer script

1. Supply a genuinely unseen design problem — resist the temptation to reuse one of the three practiced designs even in modified form.
2. During bottleneck analysis, ask which prior-week chapter the candidate is drawing the mitigation from (e.g., "is this the same stampede mechanism from Week 4?") — checking whether the material transfers across contexts rather than being tied to its original example.
3. For the behavioral round, ask for all 8 stories in one sitting rather than picking a subset — the target is 8 stories at 2 minutes each, and this is the only mock in the programme that actually tests all 8 in one sitting.
4. Score with `07-interview-readiness-rubric.md`'s System Design and Behavioral dimensions.
