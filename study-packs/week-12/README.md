# Week 12 — Full Loop Simulation

## Weekly Outcome

Four complete, timed, multi-round mock-interview loops (no new topics), a verbatim third re-run of the Day-0 diagnostic, and a completed `final-readiness-assessment.md` scoring all six `00-project/learning-roadmap.md` §8 dimensions against the §8.7 final-loop bar (≥4/5 on all six). This is Plan B's capstone week — the deliverable is evidence of readiness, not new material.

## Why This Week Matters

Every prior week added topics or vocabulary; this week adds nothing new and instead tests whether everything learned across Weeks 1-11 holds up under real interview pressure — timed, multi-round, cold, with follow-ups pushed hard. The roadmap's own framing: Week 6 asked "are you survivable," Week 9 asked "is the checkpoint diagnostic clean," Week 12 asks the final, honest question — "ready for a real loop, or not, and specifically where not."

## Prerequisites

All of Weeks 1-11. This week assumes fluent recall across the full topic register, not fresh study of any one area — if a specific loop round exposes a genuine gap, that's the point of running it now rather than in a real interview.
