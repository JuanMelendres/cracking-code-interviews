## Why no `week-01/`, `week-02/`, … structure

Unlike [`junior-to-mid/`](../junior-to-mid/README.md) and [`mid-to-senior/`](../mid-to-senior/README.md), this pack has no per-week subdirectories, by design. Its source learning path, [`syllabus/00-overview/learning-paths/senior-interview-refresh.md`](../../syllabus/00-overview/learning-paths/senior-interview-refresh.md), is itself a short (3–5 day) recall rotation, not a multi-week onboarding program — there is no new-topic sequence to schedule week by week. The source path states its own reason for not enumerating topics directly:

> "This path is deliberately not an enumerated topic list — re-listing every topic here would duplicate the cheat-sheet and flashcard layers that already exist specifically for this purpose (see this project's own duplication rule)."

This pack's single `README.md` follows the same principle: it operationalizes the source path's rotation against real `cheat-sheets/` and `flashcards/` contents rather than restating a topic list or inventing a weekly cadence the source material doesn't have.
